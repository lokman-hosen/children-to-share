About Our Organization

Sunshine Elementary School has been serving the community since 2005, providing quality education to children from diverse backgrounds. We believe in fostering not just academic excellence but also compassion and community spirit.

Through ThreeWish, we connect families in need with generous donors who can help make children's wishes come true. Whether it's school supplies, winter clothing, or books for our library, every donation makes a significant impact on our students' lives.
